Sort Lines A to Z: Alphabetize Lists & Paragraphs, a Google Docs™ add-on run by Bobby Landerman. Effective 12 November 2026.

In short

The add-on reads and rearranges the lines you select, inside your document, while you use it. Your document's content is not stored and is not sent anywhere.

What the add-on can access

Only the document you use it in ("View and manage documents that this application has been installed in") and the ability to show its menu and dialogs. It cannot see your other files, your email or your contacts. When you install from the Google Workspace Marketplace, Google also lists its standard sign-in permissions (your name and email address); the add-on never reads or stores them.

What is processed, and where

  • Your document's text and formatting is read and rearranged by the add-on's code, which runs on Google's servers as part of Google Docs, only when you choose a menu item. It is not saved, copied elsewhere or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your license key, if you buy the paid extras, is saved in your own Google account's storage for this add-on (Apps Script user properties) so it works in all your documents. We can't read it from there.
  • Usage counts. To know whether the add-on is used, it records events such as "sort, A to Z, 11 to 50 lines, 1.2 seconds, finished" in the add-on's own Google Cloud logs. These records contain no document text, document names or IDs, and no names or email addresses. Google's Apps Script platform adds its own technical details to each log entry.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe receives the details you give at checkout (such as your email address and card) under Stripe's privacy policy. We see the record of your purchase (amount, date, email address) in our Stripe account and use it only to provide your license and support, and for tax and accounting records.

Google user data

Sort Lines A to Z's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or let anyone read it.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: sortlines-support@googlegroups.com. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.

How do I sort a list?

  1. Select the lines, list items or paragraphs you want to sort.
  2. Choose Extensions > Sort Lines A to Z > Sort A to Z (or Sort Z to A).
  3. Done. To go back, press Undo once (Ctrl+Z, or ⌘Z on a Mac).

Why did I get a message instead of a sorted list?

The add-on never changes your document unless it can sort it safely. The message names the reason and what to do. The usual ones are: nothing was selected; only one line was selected; the selection crosses several table cells (select lines inside one cell, or use Sort table rows by a column); or a line contains a footnote, smart chip or drawing that would be lost if the line moved.

What stays the same when I sort?

Bullets, numbering, checklist ticks and nested items (they move with the item above them, and are sorted too). Bold, italics and links stay on the same words. Headings stay where they are, and the lines under each heading are sorted on their own. Blank lines keep their place.

I'm in Suggesting mode. Why was the sort applied directly?

Google Docs applies add-on changes directly, even in Suggesting mode. Undo reverses it.

How do the paid extras work, and how do I get my key again?

The paid extras cost $19 once, with no subscription: sort ignoring "a", "an" or "the", sort by last name, remove duplicate lines, shuffle, and sort table rows by a column. After paying you see your license key on a page you can bookmark; reopening that page shows the same key. Enter it with Extensions > Sort Lines A to Z > Enter license key…. It works in every document you open with the same Google account. Lost the key and the page? Write to support with your receipt.

Is there a size limit?

Up to 500 lines at a time (about 15 seconds at the top end). For a longer list, sort it in parts.
